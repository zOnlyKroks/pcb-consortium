# NixiClockV1 case

A base tray and a flat lid. The six IN-16 tubes stand up through the lid, and all 185 V copper stays inside.

- Outside: **134.4 × 66.4 × 26.5 mm**. The tubes stand about 30 mm proud of the lid.
- Generated by `case.py` (CadQuery 2.8). Change the parameters at the top and re-run it.
- The board geometry (mounting holes, tube positions, USB-C, WJ500V) is copied from `NixiClockV1.kicad_pcb`.
- The script checks the fit against a dummy PCB with its tall parts (C106, WJ500V, L101, ESP32, USB-C, buttons) and the tubes: no interference.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `case_base.step`, `case_lid.step` | CNC quotes, editing in Fusion 360 / FreeCAD |
| `case_base.stl`, `case_lid.stl` | 3D printing. The lid STL is already flipped top-down. |
| `case_assembly.step` | Base, lid, dummy PCB and tubes, for checking the fit |
| `case_lid_lasercut.dxf` | Flat lid for 3 mm acrylic (mm, outline plus holes) |
| `preview/` | Renders |

## Openings
- **Lid:**
  - 6 × Ø12.6 mm tube holes (`TUBE_HOLE`; measure your tubes, IN-16 glass is about 11–12 mm);
  - 2 × Ø4.8 mm screwdriver holes above the WJ500V clamps;
  - 2 × Ø2.5 mm pin holes for RESET / BOOT, labelled.
- **Right wall:** 13 × 7.5 mm USB-C window, wide enough for normal cable overmolds.
- **Back wall:** WJ500V window. Wires go in from the back; tighten the clamps through the lid holes.
- **Floor:** finger-safe 1.6 mm slots under the HV converter, and 4 recesses for 10 mm self-adhesive rubber feet.

## Hardware
- 4 × M3 × 12 socket-head screws. They go in from below, through the base column and the PCB, into the lid boss, and clamp everything at once.
- 4 × M3 heat-set inserts (for 4.0 mm holes, about 5.7 mm long) in the lid bosses, pressed in with a soldering iron.
- 4 × 10 mm rubber feet.

## Assembly
1. Solder the tubes so the glass starts about 5 mm above the PCB. The lid underside sits 13 mm above the PCB.
2. Put the PCB on the base columns.
3. Fit the lid over the tubes. Its lip locates it in the base.
4. Screw everything together from below.

## 3D printing
- **Material:** PETG or ASA (PLA works too; the board makes less than 2 W of heat).
- **Settings:** 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 20 % infill.
- **Orientation:** both parts print without supports. Print the base floor-down and the lid top-down, as in the STL.
- **Bridges:** the USB-C and WJ500V windows bridge 13 mm or less.

## CNC
- Send the STEP files and set `INSERT = False` first. The lid bosses then get 2.5 mm tap-drill holes; ask for an M3 thread.
- All inside vertical corners are R3 mm.
- The base needs a second setup for the two wall windows. Only the lid is single-sided.
- **Plastic (POM / ABS):** cheapest, and WiFi works.
- **Aluminium blocks the ESP32-C3 antenna.** Use a plastic lid, or set `METAL = True`: that opens the lid above the antenna (back-right corner) for a plastic window. A metal case also needs grounding near 185 V.
- **Cheapest nice-looking option:** a printed base plus `case_lid_lasercut.dxf` cut from 3 mm acrylic (smoked or black). Use 4 × M3 13 mm female-female standoffs between the PCB and the lid instead of the printed bosses.
