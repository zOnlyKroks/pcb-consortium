ADS1293 RLD OSCILLATION - ROOT CAUSE IDENTIFIED
================================================

PROBLEM: RLD capacitor is 17 pF instead of 1.5 nF (1500 pF)

This is 88x SMALLER than the datasheet specification!


WHY THIS CAUSES OSCILLATION:
----------------------------

The RLD (Right Leg Drive) circuit is a negative feedback loop:
1. ADS1293 measures common-mode voltage on IN1/IN2/IN3
2. RLD amplifier inverts and drives it back to IN4 (RL electrode)
3. This cancels common-mode noise (50/60 Hz interference)

The capacitor stabilizes this feedback loop by providing:
- Low-pass filtering
- Phase compensation
- Prevents oscillation


IMPACT OF WRONG CAPACITOR VALUE:
---------------------------------

YOUR PCB: 1 MΩ + 17 pF
- RC time constant = 17 µs
- Cutoff frequency = ~9.4 kHz
- Result: 90x HIGHER bandwidth than intended

DATASHEET SPEC: 1 MΩ + 1.5 nF
- RC time constant = 1.5 ms
- Cutoff frequency = ~106 Hz
- Result: Stable RLD feedback

With 90x higher bandwidth, the RLD feedback loop is UNSTABLE and oscillates!


THIS EXPLAINS ALL YOUR SYMPTOMS:
---------------------------------

✓ Why RLD_CN = 0x04 (TI spec, active RLD) causes oscillation
✓ Why RLD_CN = 0x00 (disabled RLD) has growing amplitude
✓ Why RLD_CN = 0x0F might still be unstable
✓ The 30-second cycling pattern (slow oscillation buildup)
✓ Signal works for a few seconds then "goes to shit"
✓ Amplitude cycles: 0.5 mV → 10 mV → 0.5 mV → repeat


SOLUTIONS:
----------

OPTION 1: Fix the Hardware (BEST SOLUTION)
==========================================
Replace the 17 pF capacitor with 1.5 nF (1500 pF)

Component specifications:
- Value: 1.5 nF (also marked as 152, 1n5, or 1500pF)
- Type: Ceramic capacitor (X7R or C0G/NP0)
- Package: 0603 or 0805 SMD
- Voltage rating: 16V or higher

After replacing:
- Configure RLD_CN = 0x04 (TI datasheet specification)
- RLD will be stable and provide excellent noise rejection
- No software filtering workarounds needed


OPTION 2: Software Workaround (CURRENT APPROACH)
=================================================
Keep RLD disabled to prevent oscillation

Configuration:
- Set RLD_CN = 0x00 (RLD disabled)
- Accept higher 50/60 Hz noise in signal

Disadvantages:
- More power line interference

This is trial-and-error and won't be as good as the correct capacitor.
