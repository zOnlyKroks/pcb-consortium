CONFIGURATION SUMMARY:

3-Lead ECG Setup (per datasheet section 9.2.1):
- Channel 1: Lead I (LA - RA)
- Channel 2: Lead II (LL - RA)
- Channel 3: Lead III (LL - LA)
- Common-mode detection: Averaging IN1, IN2, IN3 (RA, LA, LL)
- RLD output: Driving RL electrode via IN4 for common-mode rejection
- Reference: Internal 2.4V
- Clock: External 4.096 MHz crystal
- Sample rate: ~85 Hz per channel (after decimation)

Data Format:
timestamp_ms, raw_value, voltage_mV

Voltage Conversion:
voltage_mV = (raw_value / 8388607.0) * 1200.0
